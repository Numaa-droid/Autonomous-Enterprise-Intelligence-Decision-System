Find and read every table in your medical report

1 · Detect — Microsoft's Table Transformer and IBM Docling's layout model find the tables, paragraphs, headings and pictures on every page. 2 · Recognise — Microsoft's Table Transformer v1.1 reads each table's rows, columns and cells (bordered or borderless) and fills them with the PDF's own text. Tables printed sideways or without a text layer, and pictures, charts and scans, are read by PaddleOCR-VL-1.6. Every lab test then comes out with its value, unit and reference range, flagged High, Low or Abnormal against the range the report prints.
